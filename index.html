Approach

How I play a frame

Same four shots on every project, whether it's a payout system or a signup flow.

  1. 1

    Read the table

    Talk to users, the business and engineering. Map what exists, what breaks and who feels it.

  2. 2

    Plan the break

    Frame the problem, map flows and edge cases, and agree on what a good outcome looks like.

  3. 3

    Play the shot

    Wireframe, prototype and test fast. Build with engineering, not just for them.

  4. 4

    Chalk the score

    Ship, measure what moved, and write down what I'd play differently next time.
