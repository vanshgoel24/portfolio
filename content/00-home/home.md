## About — Between shots

## Approach — How I play a frame
1. **Read the table** — Talk to sellers, ops and finance. Map what exists, what breaks and who feels it.
2. **Plan the break** — Frame the problem, map flows and edge cases, and agree on what a good outcome looks like.
3. **Play the shot** — Wireframe, prototype and test fast. Build with engineering, not just for them.
4. **Chalk the score** — Ship, measure what moved, and write down what I'd play differently next time.
